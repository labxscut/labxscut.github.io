The project "海洋生物来源高附加值酶制剂的创制及工业化应用" (Creation and
Industrial Application of High Value-Added Enzymes from Marine Biological
Resources) entered public notice (公示) on 2026-08-01 as a recommended entry for
the 2026 Natural Resources Science and Technology Award. South China University
of Technology is a completing unit and PI Li C. Xia is among the contributing
authors; the work builds on LabX's marine enzyme bioinformatics (sxEnzHier /
EnzHire).

Framing note for future edits: this is a NOMINATION in the public-notice stage,
not a conferred prize — the 公示 material recommends the project for the
special/first prize and cites a 2026 Chinese Society of Oceanography appraisal,
but no award result is recorded. Do not rewrite this as "won" / "received"
unless and until an award decision is published.
